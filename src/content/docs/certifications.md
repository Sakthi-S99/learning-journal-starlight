:::tip[How to add a new certification]
**Step 1** — Get your badge image. **Step 2** — add a card under the right section. **Step 3** — fill in the details, save, and push. Details below.
:::

**Step 1 — Get your badge image**

| Source | What to do |
|--------|-----------|
| **Local file** | Drop the image (`.png`, `.svg`, `.webp`) into `public/badges/` |
| **Credly** | Open your badge → Share → copy the **Image URL** and **Public URL** |
| **Guidewire / Badgr** | Open your badge → right-click the image → Copy image address |

**Step 2 — Add a card under the right section**

Use a `<Card>` from `@astrojs/starlight/components` (requires converting this file to `.mdx`), or a plain Markdown image + link:

```md
[![Guidewire Associate — BillingCenter](/badges/guidewire-associate-bc.png)](YOUR_VERIFY_URL)
```

**Step 3 — Fill in the details, save, and push.**
