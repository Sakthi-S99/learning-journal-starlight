## How to Add a New Certification

<Steps>

1. **Get your badge image**

   | Source | What to do |
   |--------|-----------|
   | **Local file** | Drop the image (`.png`, `.svg`, `.webp`) into `public/badges/` |
   | **Credly** | Open your badge → Share → copy the **Image URL** and **Public URL** |
   | **Guidewire / Badgr** | Open your badge → right-click the image → Copy image address |

2. **Add a card under the right section**

   Use a `<Card>` from `@astrojs/starlight/components`, or a plain Markdown image + link:

   ```md
   [![Guidewire Associate — BillingCenter](/badges/guidewire-associate-bc.png)](YOUR_VERIFY_URL)
   ```

3. **Fill in the details, save, and push.**

</Steps>
